### 🌱 A bit about me

I'm currently studying Information Systems at Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS), Surabaya, specializing in Digital Innovation. Most of my time goes into exploring UI/UX design, building web projects, and helping run events or creative initiatives through student organizations and campus committees.

I like turning ideas and visual concepts into working digital solutions. My involvement in organizations like the ITS Astronomy Club and various major projects started from a passion for creative media, design management, and effective digital communication. That's usually how my workflow looks: spotting a communication or design gap, then experimenting through tools like Figma, Adobe, and Canva until everything clicks.

Outside of design and code, I spend time exploring digital innovations, creative media projects, and keeping up with new tech trends—because I enjoy shaping engaging user experiences almost as much as building software.
